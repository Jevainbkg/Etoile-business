Money transfer

Send money to your family or receive a transfer, in the DRC or abroad.

  • Available networks: MoneyGram, Ria, Equity, M-Pesa and Airtel Money.
  • To send: your ID, the recipient's full name and country.
  • To receive: your ID and the transfer reference given by the sender.
  • Fees depend on the network, the amount and the country: ask us in branch or on WhatsApp.
